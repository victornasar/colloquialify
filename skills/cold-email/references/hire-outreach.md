# Hire outreach (recruiter / hiring manager)

Use when the goal is a **job reply**, not problem discovery.
Triggers: cold email a recruiter or hiring manager, intro for an open role,
“message this person about a job,” or when the user points at this framework.

This is **not** the discovery middle path. Do not invent an HVAC-style intrigue
question. Do not use open-hiring-dm’s ultra-short “want in / resume?” shape
unless the recipient already posted that applications or DMs are open (then
prefer colloquialify’s open-hiring-dm).

## Framework (keep all six beats; cut fluff inside each)

Short and sweet:

1. **Who you are** — one plain line (role + stack or focus).
2. **Why you’re emailing ME** — proof of diligence. Name their post, team,
   product, or a specific detail that shows this isn’t a blast.
3. **What you’re looking for** — proof of relevance. The role or lane, in
   ordinary words (no “req”).
4. **Link to CV / resume** — proof of excellence.
5. **Link to portfolio** — proof of creativity (site, GitHub, shipped work).
6. **Unique insight** — proof of value. One concrete observation about their
   product, team, or problem. Not mission parroting. Not “I’m passionate.”

## Shape

- **Subject** = role + light specificity (e.g. `Growth full-stack application`,
  `frontend at TypeSafe`). Intent over cleverness.
- **Body** = the six beats in order, tight paragraphs or short lines. Blank
  line before links so they breathe on mobile.
- **Audience mode** still applies: skeptic (lowercase) vs trade (normal caps).
  Muslim recipients: salaam ± short dua first when voice profile says so.
- **No em dashes.** No Hi/Hello. No sign-off name.
- **No call asks.** Links are the proof; they can reply if interested.
- **Length:** hire mail can go past 240 chars because of links. Still cut first.
  If it needs a second screen, you wrote too much.

## Cut

- Mission / passion / “join the frontier” fluff
- Recruiter jargon the writer wouldn’t say (“req,” “loop,” “top of funnel”)
- Soft chat asks (“happy to hop on a call,” “would love to connect”)
- Generic insight that could fit any company after a noun swap
- Second unique-insight paragraph (one is enough)

## Output

```
purpose: hire
mode: skeptic | trade

subject: …

body:
…

chars: N
send: their business hours (timezone if known)
```
