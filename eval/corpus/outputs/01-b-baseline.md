Coding assistants speed up some tasks. In practice, the wins show up first on boilerplate: config files, test scaffolding, repetitive refactors.

They are worse at knowing when they are wrong. Suggestions can compile, pass lint, and still do the wrong thing if nobody reads the diff carefully.

Adoption has moved from hobby experiments into company workflows. That does not settle the productivity debate. Acceptance rate is not correctness, and correctness is not value.

Use them as tools. Keep review standards high. If you do not have tests, you cannot tell whether the suggestion is right.
