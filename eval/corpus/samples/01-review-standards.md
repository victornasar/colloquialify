I keep seeing teams treat coding assistants like junior engineers. They aren't.

Last quarter I sat with three squads that turned Copilot on by default. Throughput on boilerplate went up. Review time went up too, because people stopped reading the boring parts carefully. The bugs that landed weren't exotic. They were the kind you catch when you still care about the diff.

If the tool writes the easy 40%, you still own 100% of the merge. That trade only works if review standards stay annoying.
